[ Fintech & Banking ]

Three banking brands, three challenges

One challenge from each brand: a white-label design system, a faster onboarding and an anti-fraud tool.

Overview

I designed business banking products for Framnex, Kanzum and Payler. Beyond product interfaces, I built and maintained the design system, created documents and presentations, and designed new brands and their landing pages.

Some interfaces are under NDA and are shown partially.
Role

Product Designer. Sole designer on Framnex and Kanzum; at Payler, together with the Head of Design.

Product

Business banking for three brands: Framnex and Payler in Europe, Kanzum in Asia, Africa and MENA.

Timeline

Dec 2023 – Oct 2026 · 2 yrs 11 mos

Three decisions
Decision 01 · FramnexOfficial website ↗

Building a scalable white-label banking design system

I designed the visual experience of an existing banking platform, building a design system from scratch and applying it across core banking interfaces. The system lets different banking brands adapt the product's identity within the platform's technical limits.

Problem

  1. 01No unified visual systemThe existing banking platform lacked a cohesive visual language and a consistent set of reusable interface components.
  2. 02White-label flexibilityThe product was designed for different banking brands, requiring an interface adaptable to their visual identities and color schemes without a complete redesign.
  3. 03Existing technical constraintsThe design had to fit the capabilities of the existing platform. New components and interface concepts needed to work within its technical limitations.

Solution

  1. 01Built a design system from scratchCreated a complete visual foundation, including typography, colors, buttons, input fields, forms, and reusable UI components.
  2. 02Designed for white-label adaptabilityBrand colors are separated from the rest of the interface, so standard palettes work out of the box and only unconventional colors need manual refinement.
  3. 03Applied the system across the platformDesigned the dashboard, accounts, activity, send money and exchange flows, and the back-office tools on the new system.
Decision 02 · KanzumOfficial website ↗

Reducing friction in banking onboarding

Users had to complete many registration steps before they could see the banking interface. I restructured the journey so people could explore the product first and finish the requirements later.

Problem

  1. 01Too many steps upfrontUsers went through several stages of registration screens before they could reach the banking interface.
  2. 02No product before the checksThe banking interface opened only after every check, including KYC.
  3. 03User drop-offThe lengthy registration journey created friction, with users abandoning the process before completing account creation.

Solution

  1. 01Competitive researchCompared onboarding in six banking products (Wise, Revolut, Finom, Grey, Kuda and XTransfer): screens per stage, KYC requirements and when users first get access.
  2. 02Simplified onboarding flowShortened the upfront part of the journey by moving pre-qualification and KYC to a later stage.
  3. 03Earlier product accessUsers enter the banking interface with a limited account; the limit is removed once the remaining checks are complete.
Decision 03 · PaylerOfficial website ↗

Making a complex anti-fraud system accessible

I redesigned Payler's anti-fraud administration interface. The goal was to move beyond a tool only its original author could use, and make it clear for managers and other team members responsible for administration.

Problem

  1. 01Expert dependencyThe system was primarily usable by the Head of Anti-Fraud, who had designed its underlying logic. Other team members struggled to work with it independently.
  2. 02Technical complexityComplex workflows and technical terminology made everyday administrative tasks difficult without deep domain knowledge.
  3. 03Lack of user guidanceThe interface offered little support for users unfamiliar with the system, making it harder for new administrators to complete tasks.

Solution

  1. 01Simplified workflowsWorked with the domain expert to translate complex processes into clearer actions and navigation for non-experts.
  2. 02Improved interface & guidanceApplied Payler's existing banking design system and introduced contextual hints and onboarding.
  3. 03Validated with new usersTested the redesigned interface with project managers and people from adjacent teams, who could now work as administrators on their own.

[ B2B SaaS & Cloud ]

Two cloud storage products, two stages

Building Petabox's product design from scratch and improving an established product, ColdStack.

Overview

I worked on two cloud storage products at different stages. For Petabox I built the product design from scratch: the design system and the dashboard on top of an existing technical platform. For ColdStack, an established product, I refined the administration interface and selected screens.

For both products I also designed websites, presentations and social media visuals.

Role

Product Designer, working directly with the founder of Petabox and the development team.

Product

Cloud object storage for business: Petabox and ColdStack.

Timeline

May 2022 – Present · 4 yrs 7 mos

Petabox

Building the product experience

The platform architecture was already in place, but the product had no design yet. I created the design system from scratch (visual language, typography, color palette, reusable components and interface patterns) and designed the product on top of it: dashboard, storage management, administration, billing and user management.

ColdStack

Improving an existing product

ColdStack was already an established product when I joined. I redesigned its website, refined the administration interface and selected screens, working within the existing product structure while making the interface and the brand more consistent.

Petabox · ColdStack

Product communication

Beyond the interfaces, I shaped how both products looked in marketing: ad creatives, social media posts, article covers and the Petabox sales presentation. I also designed technical documentation templates and illustrations.
